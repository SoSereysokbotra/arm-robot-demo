Model overlays

Model alignment

The servo-to-linkage angle depends on how each horn is bolted on. Offset rotates a joint in the model; flip reverses its direction. Values are stored in this browser.

J0 base speed at 100 % °/s

How fast the real base turns. Lower it if the model spins faster than the arm.

J0 base trim (real servo) cnt

Balances the real base’s two directions. Raise it to make CW faster and CCW slower. Sent to the controller, which forgets it on reboot.
